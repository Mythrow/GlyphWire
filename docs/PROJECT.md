# GlyphWire: project brief

GlyphWire is a YouTube Shorts channel about breaking world news. The name means
"wire news told through glyphs."

**Goal:** publish a video as soon as a news event breaks. Stories can come from
news, media, or wire sources, from stories the owner passes in by hand, or from
non-internet sources the owner reports directly.

**Format:** modern-futuristic motion graphics, with no voiceover and light
background music. Text and graphics move in smooth, programmatically generated
animation. Quick informative videos, not detailed explainers.

**Engine:** Remotion (React). Adobe software is not used.

**Workflow:** the owner approves and uploads each final video at first. The
pipeline becomes fully automated later, once the owner judges the pace is good
enough.

Decisions and open questions are recorded in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
